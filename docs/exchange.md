# PEX Exchange

**PEX** is the PezkuwiChain exchange, where you can trade **HEZ**, **PEZ**, and
**PEZ-20** assets such as USDT.

- Web: **[pex.network](https://pex.network/)** (also reachable at
  **exchange.pezkuwichain.io**)

## What you can do

- Trade pairs involving HEZ, PEZ, and USDT (PEZ-20)
- Deposit and withdraw assets to/from your PezkuwiChain accounts
- Convert between assets

## Non-profit fee model

PEX runs on a **cost-recovery, non-profit** basis. Rather than maximizing trading
fees, fees are set low to cover operating costs:

- **Maker:** 0%
- **Taker:** ~0.01%

Convert spreads exist primarily to cover risk, not to generate profit.

!!! note "USDT shows a PEZ-20 badge"
    On PEX, USDT issued on Pezkuwi Asset Hub is labeled **USDT · PEZ-20 (Pezkuwi
    Asset Hub)** — the same convention exchanges use for **USDT TRC-20** or
    **USDT ERC-20**. It tells you which network the token lives on. See
    [Tokens & Standards](tokens.md).

## Deposits & withdrawals

When depositing, send the asset **on the correct chain** (e.g. a PEZ-20 asset on
Asset Hub). When withdrawing, the exchange sends to the address and chain you
specify — double-check both.

!!! tip
    As with any exchange, do a small **test deposit/withdrawal** the first time you
    use a new address.
